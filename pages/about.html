About Us

Transforming Campus Events

Eventrix Campus connects students, organizers, and administrators on one platform — making KIUT events easier to plan, discover, and attend.

Get Started Contact Us

Our Mission

Every student deserves access to meaningful campus experiences.

We bridge the gap between event organizers and students through technology that makes campus life more connected, engaging, and memorable.

"Turning scattered announcements into seamless, engaging experiences that bring university communities together."

Why Eventrix Campus?

Built specifically for universities — simple for students, powerful for staff.

Student-Focused

Reg-number sign-in, one-click registration, and a clear view of upcoming campus events.

Mobile Ready

Fully responsive design — browse, register, and manage events from any device.

Secure & Private

Role-based access, session authentication, and data stored on secure campus infrastructure.

Data-Driven

Real-time registration counts, approval workflows, and reports for administrators.

Smart Notifications

Automated alerts for approvals, registrations, and event updates across all roles.

Built for Africa

Designed for African universities with local context, infrastructure, and campus culture in mind.
