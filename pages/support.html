Common Issues

Fast fixes for the most reported problems.

Ensure your reg number matches the format stored in the system (e.g. KIUT/2024/1001). Remove extra spaces and use uppercase. Contact your faculty office if your number is not yet in the database.

All organizer events require admin approval. Check the Approvals page as an admin, or wait for the campus administrator to review your submission. You'll receive a notification when approved or rejected.

Click the Refresh button on the relevant page, or hard-refresh your browser (Ctrl+Shift+R). Ensure WAMP/Apache and MySQL are running if using the local installation.

Check My Registrations in the app dashboard. If the event is missing, the registration may not have completed — try registering again while signed in.
