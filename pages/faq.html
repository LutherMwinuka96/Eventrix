Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about accounts, events, registrations, and platform features at KIUT.

Students sign in with their university registration number on the Sign In page. No password is required — your reg number is verified against the campus database.

Organizers register via the Sign Up page with email and password. Admin accounts are provisioned by the system administrator and use dedicated credentials on the staff sign-in tab.

You can publish academic seminars, workshops, sports, cultural events, club meetings, and social gatherings. All events must follow KIUT campus guidelines and require admin approval before going live.

Approved events appear on the Events page. Students browse, view details, and register with one click. Confirmation and reminders are sent through the in-app notification system.

Yes. Organizers have a dashboard showing all their events, registration counts, pending approvals, and participant lists — all in one place.

Students discover and register for events. Organizers create and manage their own events. Admins approve events, manage users, and access platform-wide reports and analytics.

All organizer-submitted events go to the Approvals queue. Campus administrators review details, venue, and capacity before publishing events to students.

Eventrix Campus is fully responsive. Access every feature from your phone browser — no separate app install needed.

Organizers can edit events from My Events before the start date. To cancel, update the event status or contact an administrator. Registered participants receive automatic notifications.
